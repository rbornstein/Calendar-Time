Things to fit in

Each open block gets a plan from this list. The longest activities go into the blocks they fit best. Leftover time then gets anything you do once a day that isn't planned yet that day, plus one session of anything set to every open block. Activities ticked as exercise are suggested at most once a day in total, so a gym day isn't also a pool day. Pick a gym under “Uber to” and the plan adds the whole trip: the pickup wait, the ride there and back with traffic for that time (from TomTom Maps), and a shower at the gym. For those, the length is your time at the gym.
